What is Unimate?

Unimate is a verified marketplace exclusively for university students. We create a safe, closed ecosystem where you can buy, sell, and exchange items with people you trust—your peers.

No outsiders, no scams, and no long-distance hassles. Delivery happens right inside your campus or university area.
